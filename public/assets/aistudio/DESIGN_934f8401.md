## Brand & Style
This design system is tailored for students engaging in spiritual wellness, daily yoga, breathwork, and scriptural study. The visual identity harmonizes the transcendent calmness of Vedic tradition with the crisp clarity required for modern academic habit tracking. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Mindful Glassmorphism & Celestial Twilight**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep twilight indigoes, meditative azure gradients, and sacred flame/marigold accents evoke tranquil dawn and twilight contemplation.
- **Surface Texture:** Multi-layered translucent glass cards featuring soft specular top-borders, backdrop diffusion (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`), and gentle inner illumination.
- **Personality:** Serene, grounded, luminous, and disciplined. It replaces clinical productivity charts with gentle affirmations, sacred geometry accents (mandalas, yantras), and tactile progress rings.
- **Audience:** Students, seekers, and practitioners balancing rigorous daily study with meditation (Dhyana), breath control (Pranayama), reflection (Svadhyaya), and selfless service (Seva).

## Colors
The color palette creates a luminous night sky, balanced by high-legibility light frosted containers:

- **Celestial Base (`celestial-midnight` & `twilight-deep`):** Deep twilight sky hues provide an immersive backdrop, enriched with radiant gradients (`linear-gradient(180deg, #102A4E 0%, #060D17 100%)`).
- **Primary Celestial Cobalt (`#2563EB`):** Used for focal action points—such as the prominent "Begin Sadhana" floating pills, interactive media play/pause triggers, and navigation highlights.
- **Secondary Mist (`#E0E7FF` / `surface-glass-solid`):** Used for the iconic card layers where structured exercise routines and book libraries are read, ensuring text contrast against rich sacred backgrounds.
- **Tertiary & Sacred Accents (`sadhana-gold` / `sacred-amber`):** Gold and warm diya flame tones mark active streaks, time counters, glowing mandala petals, and morning/afternoon salutations.
- **Mindful Category Chromas:** Soft tints used in habit category tags and duration counters:
  - `lotus-lavender` for Mantra & Japa
  - `peace-mint` for Affirmation & Pranayama
  - `flame-glow` for Swadhyaya & Sacred Texts

## Typography
**Plus Jakarta Sans** is selected across all roles to combine geometric structure with gentle humanist curves. 

- **Titles & Headings:** Crisp and peaceful with slightly tight negative letter-spacing, providing clean presence over complex translucent layers and background artwork.
- **Category & Eyebrow Badges:** Rendered in `label-caps` with uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`), establishing structured hierarchy for Vedic disciplines (e.g., `DAILY SADHANA`, `STUDY SELECTION`).
- **Body & Subtitles:** Set in medium weights with open line-heights to support clarity when reading Sanskrit terms, mantra translations, and study text excerpts.
- **Affirmations & Reflections:** Styled with `quote-italic` for reflective emphasis within devotional cards.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a touch-optimized, fluid mobile grid model centered around continuous vertical flow with nested horizontal carousels.

- **Screen Canvas:** Mobile gutters sit at `1rem` (16px) with an outer padding margin of `1.25rem` (20px). On larger devices or tablets, margins expand to `2rem` (32px) while restricting the maximum content track width to `540px` for optimal single-thumb reachability.
- **Rhythm & Stacking:** Main discipline sections (`DAILY SADHANA`, `DAILY YOGA`, `DAILY SWADHYAYA`) use `space-xl` (28px) vertical separation to prevent visual clutter and reduce cognitive fatigue.
- **Horizontal Carousels:** Category pill rails, book carousels, and quick action cards bleed to the right edge with dynamic negative margin offsets and a trailing padding spacer of `1.25rem` to cue scrollability.
- **Safe Area Insets:** Fixed bottom navigation retains a bottom safe area cushion of `1.5rem` to float smoothly above device home indicators.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids harsh drop shadows, relying instead on ambient glow, light reflection, and frosted glass layers:

1. **Base Layer (Level 0):** The deep night or twilight sky artwork featuring subtle golden sacred geometry motifs (such as Nataraja outlines or mandalas).
2. **Ambient Frosted Glass (Level 1):** Primary content cards and horizontal quick-cards use high-diffusion glass:
   - `background: rgba(229, 237, 253, 0.88)` for bright foreground cards, or `rgba(14, 36, 66, 0.65)` for deep mood panels.
   - `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`.
   - `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)` with an inner top specular glow (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`).
3. **Elevated Floating Controls (Level 2):** Primary action buttons, round circular play controls, and audio trigger discs:
   - `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`.
4. **Active Focus & Aura Rings (Level 3):** Pranayama breathing discs and diya flames cast a radial golden-azure halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 35px rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.25)`).

## Shapes
The shape language uses organic, soft contours that convey tranquility, safety, and natural rhythm:

- **Large Content Containers:** Standardized at `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for master glassmorphic workout modules and study cards.
- **Category Chips, Floating Badges & Primary Triggers:** Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`) to invite thumb interaction and convey softness.
- **Book & Lesson Thumbnails:** Set to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) with soft clipping to match physical book covers.
- **Circular Indicators:** Timers, audio icons, and step number avatars use exact 1:1 circular geometries.

## Components

### 1. Action Buttons
- **Primary Pill CTA:** Deep cobalt blue (`#2563EB`) background with white bold text, pill-shaped (`height: 48px`), accompanied by a right-facing play arrow icon. Pressed state scales smoothly to `0.97` with elevated blue aura glow.
- **Floating Icon Buttons:** Circular (`44px × 44px`) frosted glass buttons for back navigation and information overlays (`Info ⓘ`), using crisp dark gray glyphs over semi-transparent white.

### 2. Sadhana Module & Workout Glass Cards
- **Structure:** Frosted white-tinted glass panels (`border-radius: 24px`) displaying top eyebrow badges (`DAILY SADHANA`), bold titles, bulleted sub-discipline summaries (`Mantra • Aum • Affirmation • Study`), and integrated action footers with duration counters (`10 min`).
- **Watermark Motifs:** Delicate single-color deity/mandala line art embedded in the bottom-right corner at `15%` opacity.

### 3. Step & Practice List Items
- **Structure:** Segmented rows inside a shared container separated by thin translucent borders (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`).
- **Step Badges:** Left-aligned circular pill tokens (`36px × 36px`) filled with pastel category washes (lavender, soft mint, warm sand) housing bold step duration numbers.
- **Content:** Title in `title-md`, secondary Sanskrit/concept translation below in `body-sm`, and right-aligned duration (`2 min`).

### 4. Interactive Pranayama Mandala
- **Visuals:** Sacred concentric flower/mandala geometry composed of delicate white and amber vector petals that expand and contract smoothly in sync with breathing phases.
- **Phase Feedback:** Prominent uppercase tracker (`BREATHE IN`, `HOLD`, `EXHALE`) with cycle progress count (`7 of 9`) and slider pace controls.

### 5. Swadhyaya & Offline Book Cards
- **Cover Card:** Vertical card (`width: 130px`) with a `3:4` aspect ratio, rounded corners (`16px`), cover artwork, title below in `title-md` truncated to two lines, and a dot-prefixed lesson progress counter (`Lesson 2 / 68`).

### 6. Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Bar Styling:** Ultra-thin frosted glass docking bar anchored to the bottom screen, with an elevated blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`), top hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), and transparent midnight base.
- **Navigation Items:** Five fixed tab icons (Today, Practice, Library, Notepad, You). The active tab features an amber/gold sun icon with a subtle golden glow dot indicator.
